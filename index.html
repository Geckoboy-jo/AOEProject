Welcome to Ghoulle 🎃

See it for 3 seconds. Draw it from memory.

  1. 🍬🎭 Trick or treat? Treat gets you something spooky. Trick gets you something cheeky.
  2. 👀 Memorize it The image flashes for just 3 seconds. Don't blink.
  3. ✏️ Draw it Recreate it before the timer runs out.
  4. 👻 The reveal See your drawing next to the real thing. Hit Reveal early if you're done.
Drawing tips
  • Ctrl/⌘ + Z undoes your last stroke.
  • 🪣 Bucket fill colors in a whole shape.
  • Background / Foreground picks whether you draw behind or in front.
  • 💧 Dropper picks a color from your drawing.
