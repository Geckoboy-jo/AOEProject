How to play

  1. Trick or treat? Treat gets you a spooky image. Trick gets you a cheeky one.
  2. Memorize it You get 6 seconds to look at the image. Don't blink.
  3. Draw it Recreate it from memory before the timer runs out.
  4. Compare See yours next to the original, then save or share it.
Drawing tips
  • Ctrl/⌘ + Z undoes your last stroke.
  • Ctrl/⌘ + S saves your drawing.
  • Fill colors in a whole shape.
  • Background / Foreground picks whether you draw behind or in front.
  • The dropper picks a color from your drawing.
